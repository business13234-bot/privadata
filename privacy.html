Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the PrivaData Android app ("PrivaData", "the app") does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell, share or analyse it.

1. Data you store in the app

Passwords, cards, bank details, notes, IDs, Wi-Fi details, photos, videos, audio and files you add are stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is kept in the Android Keystore. We cannot see this data.

2. Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. These are used only inside the app to show which account is in use and to choose where backups go. They are not sent to us.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, the app asks Google for the drive.appdata permission. This lets the app read and write only its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive; it cannot see any of your other Drive files. Before upload, the vault is encrypted with a backup password that only you know. Neither Google nor we can read the backup without that password.

If you link a recovery Gmail, the same encrypted backup is also copied to that account's hidden app folder so you can restore with it. Removing the recovery Gmail deletes that copy.

4. Downloads

If you use the in-app downloader, the app connects directly to the web address you enter and saves the file on your device.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to Google data beyond what is described above.

6. Use of Google user data

PrivaData's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the device. You can delete the backup from inside the app, or by removing PrivaData's app data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps) and revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

PrivaData is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen or its Google Play listing.

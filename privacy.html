Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the PrivaData Android app ("PrivaData", "the app") does with your information. In short: what you save stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. Apart from Google sign-in and backup, the only connection the app can make is loading website logos, which is off unless you turn it on (section 4). We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell, share or analyse it.

1. Data you store in the app

Passwords, cards, bank details, notes, IDs, Wi-Fi details, photos, videos, audio and files you add are stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and leave it only if you turn on backup or export them yourself. The encryption key is kept in the Android Keystore. We cannot see this data.

2. Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your basic Google account details from Google and uses your name and email address. These are used only inside the app to show which account is in use and to choose where backups go. They are not sent to us.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, the app asks Google for the drive.appdata permission. This lets the app read and write only its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive; it cannot see any of your other Drive files. The backup is uploaded in encrypted form, protected by a backup password that only you know. Neither Google nor we can read the backup without that password.

If you link a recovery Gmail, the same encrypted backup is also copied to that account's hidden app folder so you can restore with it. Removing the recovery Gmail in the app also deletes that copy. If the deletion fails, for example because you are offline, the app tells you and the copy stays until you delete it as described in section 7.

4. Website logos and exported files

If you turn on Website logos, to show a logo next to a saved password or link, the app connects over HTTPS to that website, which it identifies from the web address or title you saved, and to any address the site gives for its icon. As with any web visit, those servers can see your IP address and that a request was made. Your usernames, passwords and other saved details are not sent. Logos are kept encrypted on your device. This is off by default and happens only if you turn it on in Security → Website logos; you can turn it off again at any time.

If you export a photo, video or file from the vault, the app saves an unencrypted copy to your phone's shared storage, in a PrivaData folder. Links you open are handled by your browser or another app.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to Google data beyond what is described above.

6. Use of Google user data

PrivaData's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or signing out deletes the vault from the device. Files you exported stay on your phone until you delete them. Turning backup off or uninstalling does not delete an existing backup. To delete it, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose to delete PrivaData's hidden app data. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

PrivaData is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen or its Google Play listing.

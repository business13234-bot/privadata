Your private vault, on your phone.

PrivaData is an Android app that keeps passwords, bank cards, notes, IDs, Wi-Fi details, photos, videos, audio and files locked and encrypted on your device.

Encrypted on device

Everything you save is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held in the Android Keystore.

Locked by you

A PIN or fingerprint is needed to open the app, and each section can have its own lock.

Your own Google Drive

Optional backup goes to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, encrypted with a password only you know.

Recovery Gmail

Link a second Gmail so you can restore your vault even if you lose the main account.

No PrivaData servers

PrivaData has no servers of its own and no ads or tracking. Your data stays on your phone and, only if you turn backup on, in your own Google Drive.
